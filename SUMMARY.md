# The Open Framework — Summary

*A single-page introduction. Share this when the full document is too long for the moment.*

---

**The premise:**
The nation-state model is failing to address planetary-scale challenges. Humanity is one tribe. The coordination infrastructure to operate at global scale without central authority now exists. We should use it.

**The proposal:**
An open-source, decentralized, self-evolving governance framework that:

- Distributes influence by **reputation, not wealth** — non-transferable, earned through verified contributions, decays with inactivity
- Uses **Bitcoin as the neutral global settlement layer** — borderless, finite, permissionless
- Enables a coordinated **Great Conversion** — a voluntary redenomination of fiat into satoshis, with a **Universal Basic Endowment** ensuring no verified human falls below a dignity floor
- Replaces survival-coerced work with **chosen contribution**, cooperative ownership, and recognition of care work
- Dissolves nation-state borders through parallel systems — including **Framework Personhood** for refugees, stateless persons, and climate migrants
- Operates a **decentralized World Court** whose first mandate is the **Universal Carceral Review** — independent re-examination of every incarcerated human on the planet
- Runs a **Global Law Blockchain** powering an **Exoneration Pipeline** for the wrongly imprisoned and a **Rehabilitative Justice** paradigm with time-bound Reintegration Contracts
- Addresses enforcement through an **Enforcement Working Group** that collects, compares, and standardizes the best practices humanity has developed for preventing and responding to harm — turning enforcement from a power problem into a coordination problem
- Extends personhood across the lifecycle — **children, future generations, disabled people, indigenous peoples, ecosystems and non-human entities, and the dying** — with protections, standing, and voice
- Governs **the commons** — atmosphere, oceans, freshwater, pollinators, genetic diversity, spectrum, orbital space, night sky, knowledge — as collective inheritance rather than property
- Commits to **peace, disarmament, and the end of war** — with categorical rejection of weapons of mass destruction, gradual demilitarization, and structured support for conflict zones
- Establishes **Personal Data Sovereignty** as a fundamental human right — a self-custodial Personal Data Vault where every individual owns their medical, financial, legal, and biometric records; grants granular, time-bounded, revocable access to services; and holds a transparent on-chain audit trail of every access ever made to their data
- Uses **transparent smart contracts, decentralized identity, and zero-knowledge proofs** — privacy and accountability without centralized gatekeepers
- Governs **advanced AI and AGI** as a planetary commons, favoring safety over capability, open development over concentrated power, alignment as precondition
- Runs on **quadratic voting and delegated proof-of-stake** to prevent any faction from dominating decisions
- Invites a voluntary **10% pledge** of time or resources from those who choose to participate
- **Amends itself** through supermajority community consensus
- **Names its own limitations honestly** — documenting failure modes, co-option risks, and premises that could be wrong

**What it is not:**
- Not a utopia. A set of coordination mechanisms.
- Not a revolution. Parallel systems that make the old ones irrelevant.
- Not a cryptocurrency project. Bitcoin is a tool, not the mission.
- Not a guarantee. An attempt.
- Not owned. Released to the public domain under CC0.
- Not finished. Version 0.1. The next version is yours to write.
